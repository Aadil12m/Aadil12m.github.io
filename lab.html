Reconfigurable Piezo-Jet Robot

Six hinged jet modules that fold into a box to swim and open up to hunt.

With a small team under a professor, I'm recreating the robot from Zhang et al. (2025, Sensors & Actuators A, vol. 388). The box moves along any axis by firing the module on the opposite face. We're also designing a better signal generator and amplifier to drive the piezos.

  • MPJDU moduleOne of six, joined by hinges
  • Two formsSubmerged form swims, hunting form opens to trap
  • BNO086 IMUMeasures each module's thrust

Elephant-Trunk End Effector

A soft, biomimetic gripper modeled on the finger-like tip of an elephant's trunk.

I worked in a soft robotics lab on the design of this end effector. On the vision side, I built a PyTorch pipeline that pairs YOLOv8-pose keypoint detection with Segment Anything.
